For FPL Analytica leadership review

This is a public campaign-page draft. It does not create accounts or run an acquisition campaign.

Decision needed: free-access end date

The supplied product screens show premium free until 30 September. The campaign proposes extending this to 20 October 2026. Leadership needs to approve the extension or choose another end date. No decision is assumed.

If approved, update actual premium access and the date shown across the product, this page and campaign messages before promotion. Guest and Free access remain afterwards, with no automatic charge.

Missing piece: a completed transfer comparison

The Transfer Planner image shows only the starting screen. We still need a real example showing a loaded team, one valid alternative and the useful difference in the result.

Assign an owner to record the planned 40-second demonstration. A 12–15-second excerpt can become this page’s teaser. Use current figures and a team we have permission to show. These still images do not replace that demonstration.

Before the campaign goes live

Connect and test the signup-to-planner journey, then verify consent and activity counting on a phone. The fixture comparison is supporting evidence; it is not presented as a premium-only feature.
